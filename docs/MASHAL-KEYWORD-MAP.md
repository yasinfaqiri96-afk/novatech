The `/mashal` shorthand permanently redirects to the Dari product owner. The
English and Pashto pages have their own canonicals and reciprocal `fa-AF`,
`ps-AF`, `en-AF`, and `x-default` alternates.

Search results showed a distinct Afghanistan-market intent for HRMIS, attendance
and payroll. Those intents are deliberately not assigned to Mashal URLs in this
release: the source snapshot available for verification supports employee and
salary transactions, but not the full attendance/leave/payroll scope described
in the missing Phase 1 evidence package. Publishing dedicated pages now would
create unsupported claims and thin intent overlap.
